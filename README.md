## Server Highlights

- **Base Building & Crafting:** Custom base building with RagBasebuilding, CodeLocks, and cabins to build and secure your dream survival FOB
- **Expanded Cooking & Items:** Cookz mod integration, custom furniture, and quality-of-life items like Zen's Swiss Knives and workbenches
- **Vehicles & Mobility:** Traverse land, snow, and water with Bicycles, Motorcycles, Snowmobiles, and Jetskis
- **Balanced Economy:** Refined loot tables, money/rubles system, and carefully tuned spawn rates for a rewarding survival loop
- **QoL Improvements:** 3rd & 1st Person gameplay, smooth performance, active community support, and zero fluff
